Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

Ever Roll Fund is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. Accessibility is central to our mission, and we are continually improving the user experience for everyone by applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Our commitment

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

Ongoing efforts

We regularly review our website to identify and address potential accessibility barriers. As technology and standards evolve, we work to maintain and improve the accessibility of our content and services.

Feedback and contact
