# Ever Roll Fund

**"Disability doesn't mean inability"**

Website for the Ever Roll Fund, a nonprofit dedicated to improving the quality of life for those with a physical disability by providing resources to access medical innovation and funding for devices insurance won't cover.

## Files

- index.html - The single-page website (HTML + embedded CSS).
- logo.png - The Ever Roll Fund logo.

## Sections

- Hero with logo, tagline, and calls to action
- Mission statement
- The Problem (funding gap and our solution)
- Our Story (founder Kortlund Natterstad)
- Get Involved (For Those in Need / For Investors)
- Contact information
