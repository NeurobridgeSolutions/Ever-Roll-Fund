EVER ROLL FUND

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Ever Roll Fund, Inc. ("Ever Roll Fund," "we," "us," or "our") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization committed to protecting the privacy of our donors, volunteers, grant applicants, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to information we collect through our website, our grant application, at events, and through our programs and communications. By using our website or providing information to us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically.

Information you provide to us, including:

  • Contact details (name, email address, mailing address, phone number)
  • Donation and payment information (billing address and the details needed to process a gift; see Section 5)
  • Grant application information (see Section 4)
  • Information you share when you register for an event, sign up for our newsletter, volunteer, or contact us
  • Any information you choose to include in messages, forms, surveys, or testimonials

Information collected automatically when you visit our website, including:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and the dates and times of your visits
  • Referring website or source
  • Information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 7)

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process donations and send tax receipts and acknowledgments
  • Review grant applications and administer grants
  • Communicate with you about our programs, events, campaigns, and impact
  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Manage volunteers, registrations, and events
  • Improve our website, communications, and operations
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and reporting obligations
  • Protect against fraud, unauthorized transactions, and other liability

We will only use your information for the purposes for which it was collected, unless we reasonably consider that we need to use it for a related and compatible purpose.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Grant payments: If your grant is approved, we share your name, the item, and the quote with the equipment manufacturer or DME supplier we pay, only as needed to verify and pay for the item (see Section 4).
  • Service providers: With trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf, such as payment processors, our online form processor, email and CRM platforms, and website hosting, who are obligated to protect your information and use it only to provide those services.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Ever Roll Fund, the people we serve, or others.
  • Organizational changes: In connection with a merger, reorganization, or transfer of the organization's assets, consistent with this policy.
  • With your consent: In any other case where you have given us permission.

4. Grant applications

When you apply for an equipment grant, you may give us information about your spinal cord injury, the equipment you need, your insurance decision, other funding, and your situation. Your application is read by the Ever Roll Fund review committee.

  • Verification: We may contact the equipment manufacturer or DME supplier, and any clinician you name, to confirm the item and price. You authorize this on the application.
  • Payment: Grants are paid directly to the equipment manufacturer or DME supplier, never to the individual. We share only what is needed to pay them.
  • Form processing: Applications and website messages are transmitted through a third-party form service (currently Formspree) that delivers them to us.
  • Saved progress: Your in-progress application is saved only in your own browser until you submit it.
  • Health information: Ever Roll Fund is a grant fund, not a healthcare provider, health plan, or insurer, so the information you give us is not covered by HIPAA. We protect it anyway and use it only to review and administer your grant.

5. Donations and payment processing

Donations made through our website are processed by third-party payment processors (such as Zeffy). We do not store full credit or debit card numbers on our servers. Your payment information is transmitted directly to and handled by the payment processor in accordance with its privacy policy and applicable security standards (including PCI-DSS). We retain donation records (such as amount, date, and contact details) as required for tax, accounting, and acknowledgment purposes.

6. Email and communications

When you subscribe to our communications or make a donation, we may send you updates, newsletters, and appeals. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or by contacting us at info@everrollfund.org. Even if you opt out of marketing emails, we may still send you transactional messages, such as donation receipts, grant decisions, or responses to your inquiries.

7. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use it. We may use analytics services to measure traffic. You can set your browser to refuse some or all cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent; some parts of our website may not function properly without them.

Do Not Track: Our website does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

8. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to other websites and services that we do not operate, including our donation processor. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any website you visit.

9. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, or reporting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or de-identify it.

11. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 except through a parent or guardian applying on a child's behalf. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to know what we collect, to request access, correction, or deletion, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell your personal information. Residents of the EU/EEA and UK may also have the right to restrict or object to processing and to data portability, and where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@everrollfund.org. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through our website or by other appropriate means. Your continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
