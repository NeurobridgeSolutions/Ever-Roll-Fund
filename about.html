About Ever Roll Fund

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Ever Roll Fund, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that makes equipment grants for people with spinal cord injuries in the United States. We cover the gap between what insurance pays and what a wheelchair, seating system, or other mobility and accessibility equipment costs.

Our mission

To improve the quality of life for those with a physical disability by providing resources to access medical innovation and funding for devices insurance won't cover, so the disabled can regain independence and return to normalcy.

At a glance

  • Legal name: Ever Roll Fund, Inc., a Florida nonprofit corporation
  • Founded: June 25, 2026, in Fort Myers, Florida
  • Tax status: 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS, exempt effective June 25, 2026 (EIN 42-3511117). Determination letter available on request.
  • State registration: Florida Registration #CH85847
  • Who we serve: people with spinal cord injuries in all fifty states, D.C., and U.S. territories

How our grants work

  • Free to apply. No tax returns or pay stubs required.
  • We fund named items with a price quote, after insurance has denied or partly covered them, or when there is no insurance.
  • Grants are paid directly to the equipment manufacturer or DME supplier, never to the individual.
  • Applicants choose their own equipment and supplier. We do not accept anything of value from manufacturers or suppliers in exchange for referrals.
  • Grants are made at the discretion of the board. Our review criteria are published on the grant application page.
