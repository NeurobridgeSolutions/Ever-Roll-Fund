Our Mission

To improve the quality of life for those with a physical disability by providing resources to access medical innovation and funding for devices insurance won't cover, so the disabled can regain independence and return to normalcy.

The Problem

For many patients, the hardest part isn't finding the right equipment — it's paying for it.

The funding gap

Even when insurance covers a device, patients are often left with a large out-of-pocket payment. The equipment that restores independence is frequently the equipment that's hardest to afford.

Our solution

We make finding the grants that help pay for assistive devices far easier. Ever Roll Fund connects people to resources so they can access the technology they need to live independently.

Our Story

Kortlund Natterstad

Co-Founder, Ever Roll Fund

Kortlund Natterstad has always been driven by a desire to help others. That drive first led him into medical device sales, where he supported surgeons and patients inside the operating room. His life shifted suddenly after a serious off-roading accident left him with a spinal cord injury affecting everything from his chest down. After two surgeries ten days apart and six weeks of intensive rehabilitation at Craig Hospital, Kort began navigating his new reality — learning to adapt, accept support, and rebuild his sense of purpose. His wife, Natalia, has been an integral part of that journey, supporting him daily.

Kort re-entered the medical field through spinal cord injury research with the North American Spinal Cord Injury Consortium (NASCIC), where he continues to consult on corporate sponsor engagement and member education. He also consults with UI Medical (QuickChange Wrap for men) and Symmetric Designs, helping them shape their messages to include perspectives from individuals with lived experience. Today, he co-leads NeuroBridge Solutions alongside Natalia — a venture that brings both clinical insight and lived experience to individuals and organizations through consulting, advocacy, and education.

Kort continues to approach life with resilience, a strong support system, and the belief in taking things one day at a time. He enjoys playing adaptive pickleball, working out in the pool, and spending time with his lovely wife, Natalia. He is a die-hard Minnesota Vikings fan with the goal of seeing them play in every stadium and has been to 16 so far. His motto guides both his work and the encouragement he offers others as they adjust to life after injury.
