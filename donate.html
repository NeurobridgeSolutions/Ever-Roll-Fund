Tax-deductible 501(c)(3) gift

Give mobility. Give independence.

Insurance often stops short of the wheelchairs, exoskeletons, and adaptive equipment people need to live fully. Your gift helps close that gap.

Where your gift goes

1

Equipment grants

We fund the gap between what insurance covers and the cost of life-changing mobility technology.

2

Paid directly to the manufacturer

Grant funds go straight to the equipment manufacturer, never to individuals, so every dollar is tied to a real device.

3

A fund that keeps giving

Gifts help build a sustainable resource for the disabled community, not a one-time payout.

No fees taken from your gift. Donations are processed by Zeffy, which charges Ever Roll Fund no platform or card fees. You will receive an emailed tax receipt right away.
